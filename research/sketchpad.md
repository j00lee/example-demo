# Sketchpad

**Category:** General-purpose browser drawing application
**Researched:** 2026-09-22
**Primary sources:**
- https://sketch.io/sketchpad/

## Snapshot

Sketchpad presents itself as a free online drawing application for all ages. It competes through breadth and approachability, supporting both artwork creation and sharing/export.

## Relevant capabilities

- Browser-based drawing for general audiences
- Digital artwork creation
- Sharing workflows
- Export to JPEG, PNG, SVG, and PDF
- A broader toolset than a pencil-only canvas

## Competitive lesson

Export format coverage is visible value: users quickly understand what they can do with a drawing after making it. Sketchpad also shows the appeal of friendly language and an accessible starting point for non-specialist users.

## Opportunity for this app

A focused canvas can differentiate through speed and low cognitive load instead of matching Sketchpad's breadth. PNG export is a likely early feature because it preserves the simple mental model while making the result useful outside the app.
