## Snapshot

Excalidraw owns the hand-drawn diagramming niche. Its visual style makes rough sketches feel intentional, while the product supports collaboration, local-first behavior, sharing, and export.

## Relevant capabilities

- Infinite canvas with hand-drawn visual treatment
- Free draw, eraser, shapes, lines, arrows, and text
- Images, shape libraries, dark mode, localization
- Undo/redo, zoom, and panning
- PNG, SVG, clipboard, and open JSON export
- Offline-capable PWA, autosave, encrypted collaboration, and shareable links

## Extensibility signal

The editor is available as the `@excalidraw/excalidraw` package. The project describes the editor as customizable and open source, making it a strong reference for an embeddable canvas rather than only a hosted web app.

## Competitive lesson

The hand-drawn visual language is a product feature, not decoration. Excalidraw also shows that export and recovery matter almost as much as drawing once a canvas becomes something users trust.

## Opportunity for this app

Stay materially simpler than Excalidraw by making the first interaction immediate and keeping the tool vocabulary small. A later differentiator could be a distinctive visual mark, frictionless save/export, or a deliberately local-first workflow.
